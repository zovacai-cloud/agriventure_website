Traditional Extraction

The Ancient Art of Wood-Pressing

Our oils are extracted in-house using the traditional wood-press (kachi ghani) method, preserving the seeds' natural nutrients, aroma, and flavour. This slow, natural process ensures purity and uncompromising quality.

Unlike refined oils that undergo high-heat processing, chemical extraction, and bleaching, our wood-pressed oils are purely mechanical. The wooden press (kachi ghani) applies gentle pressure at low temperatures, ensuring that the oil retains its natural antioxidants, vitamins, and essential fatty acids.

The result is oil that not only tastes better and smells richer, but also delivers superior nutritional value — supporting heart health, boosting immunity, and enhancing the flavour of every dish you prepare.

Health Benefits

Why Choose Wood-Pressed & Cold-Pressed?

Better Taste and Aroma

Wood and cold pressing preserves the natural taste and aroma of ingredients, resulting in richer, more authentic flavours. Refined products often lose their essence and need artificial flavourings.

No Harmful Additives

Mass production and conventional methods often use chemicals like hexane or heat, degrading product quality. Our wood-pressed and cold-pressed products are chemical-free, ensuring pure, natural ingredients.

Nutrient Retention

Conventional processing often uses high heat, destroying essential nutrients. Wood-pressed and cold-pressed methods retain more vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants.

Higher Antioxidant Content

Wood and cold pressing preserve vital antioxidants often lost in refining and high-heat processing. These antioxidants combat free radicals, promoting health and reducing chronic disease risk.
